Schools
Reinventing
Counties

A live Minecraft Education world covering the state of California. Students explore a digital map of their county, investigate a real local challenge — housing, transportation, sustainability — and design a solution.

Teams of four work with their teacher to build and document their solution, then enter the Battle of the Counties. No prior game experience required. The worlds come built. The thinking is up to your students.

Three Pillars,
One Program

01

STEM & Digital Literacy

Spatial reasoning, coding logic, and engineering design in a native digital environment. Students think like engineers while building real solutions.

02

Civic Engagement

Systems thinking, community problem-solving, and public policy exploration through authentic local challenges and real decision-makers.

03

Academic Esports

Real-time collaboration, consensus-building, and high-stakes presentation skills — in a competitive format that students actually want to win.

Battle of
the Counties

A three-round statewide civics and esports competition. The best teams from each county advance toward a live championship — and the chance to pitch their ideas to the leaders who can act on them.

Round 01

Virtual Qualifiers

Opens Sept 22

Teams build a solution to a real community challenge and submit a video walkthrough. One team of four per school. Judges select top teams from each county to advance.

Round 02

County Finals

Dec 4–5

Top teams compete live at their county office of education. Timed build events, presentations to judges and local civic leaders. Winners are crowned County Champions.

Round 03

Statewide Championship

Jan 9, 2027

County Champions face off at the Richard Nixon Presidential Library in Yorba Linda. Projects are presented to state educators, community leaders, and real decision-makers.

This Model Works

Jacksonville, FL

Riverfront Redesign

Students redesigned a riverfront park during an actual city redesign. Their Minecraft builds were shared with urban planners and influenced real design conversations — going from game world to city council agenda.

New York City

Kingsbridge Armory

Students reimagined a dormant 25+ year-old armory. High engagement through civic imagination, with students presenting their ideas directly at City Hall to real decision-makers.

Calgary, Canada

Accessible District

Students partnered with an accessibility advocate to redesign an entertainment district for disabled community members — going from building in Minecraft to advocating in person.
