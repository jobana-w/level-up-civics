Elevation

#elevation

Five levels of depth. Flat (0) is the default for all bordered cards. Elevation is additive — use sparingly for focus, not decoration.

Level 0 Flat --elev-0
Level 1 Resting --elev-1
Level 2 Raised --elev-2
Level 3 Floating --elev-3
Level 4 Overlay --elev-4
